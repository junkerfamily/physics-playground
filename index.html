Why squeezing heats it

A molecule that hits an advancing piston bounces off faster, like a tennis ball off a swung racket. Every bounce adds energy, and that extra speed is the heat.

Why the blast feels cold

Pressure turns random jostling into a shared rush in one direction. Molecules can be moving fast and still be cold if their neighbors travel alongside them. Step 2 matters too: without shedding heat first, the blast starts out much hotter. This is a 2D teaching model, so its temperatures are illustrative.
